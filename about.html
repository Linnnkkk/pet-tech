About PetGearLab

Our Mission

PetGearLab helps pet owners find the best smart devices for their furry companions. We research, compare, and review pet cameras, automatic feeders, GPS trackers, and other smart pet technology so you can make informed decisions without spending hours comparing specs.

How We Test

Every product we recommend goes through our evaluation process:

  • Research: We analyze product specifications, manufacturer claims, and technical documentation
  • User Reviews: We aggregate and analyze hundreds of verified customer reviews across multiple platforms
  • Comparison: We compare products head-to-head on features, reliability, value, and real-world usability
  • Updates: We revisit our recommendations regularly as new products launch and firmware updates change the experience

Editorial Independence

Our recommendations are based on research and analysis, not advertiser pressure. We rate products on their merits — price, features, reliability, and user satisfaction. A higher commission on one product will never influence our ranking of it versus a competitor.

Affiliate Disclosure

PetGearLab participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program. When you click a product link on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

This is how we keep the site running. It does not affect our rankings or reviews. We would make the same recommendations regardless of whether affiliate commissions are available.
