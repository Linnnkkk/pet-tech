Same food vs different food: If all cats eat the same diet, separate feeders in different rooms is the cheapest solution. If cats need different diets (weight management, prescription, kitten vs adult), SureFeed Microchip is the only real answer.

Competition level: Observe your cats during mealtime. If they peacefully share, one large feeder may work. If one cat pushes others away, you need separate feeders or microchip access control.

Budget reality: Feeding 3 cats with different diets properly costs $450 (3× SureFeed). Feeding 3 cats the same food costs $100-200 (2-3× WOPET or PETLIBRO). Know what you need before buying.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stop my cats from eating each other's food?

Three solutions work: (1) SureFeed Microchip Feeder — only opens for the right cat. (2) Separate feeders in different rooms — cats naturally spread out. (3) Staggered meal times — feed one cat, then the other 15 minutes later. For different diets, SureFeed is the only reliable solution.

How many feeders do I need for 3 cats?

If all cats eat the same food, one large-capacity feeder (PETLIBRO Granary) may work if cats don't compete aggressively. For peaceful multi-cat homes, one feeder per 2 cats with separate feeding areas works. For different diets, one SureFeed per cat.

Can one automatic feeder feed two cats?

Yes, if they eat the same food and don't compete aggressively. Schedule multiple small meals throughout the day so both cats get their share. Watch for one cat dominating the feeder. If that happens, you need separate feeders.

What's the cheapest way to feed multiple cats automatically?

Two WOPET feeders ($50 each = $100 total) in separate rooms. Simple, reliable, and affordable. For cats that eat the same food, this is 90% as effective as the SureFeed solution at 1/3 the price.
