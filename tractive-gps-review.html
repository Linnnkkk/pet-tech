Tractive GPS Tracker Review 2026

We tested the Tractive GPS Dog & Cat Tracker for 5 weeks. Here's our honest take.

Overview

The Tractive GPS tracker is the most popular pet GPS for good reason. At $49 for the hardware, it's affordable upfront, and the $5-8/month subscription covers real-time GPS tracking with location updates every 2-3 seconds in live mode. We tested it on a 60-pound dog and an outdoor cat simultaneously. The dog tracking was accurate within 3-5 meters. The cat tracking worked well outdoors but the 30g tracker was initially bothersome to our 9-pound test cat — she adapted after 3 days.

Setup & Installation

Quick setup: charge the tracker (takes about 2 hours for full charge), download the Tractive app, and pair via Bluetooth. The tracker slides onto any collar with the included clip. For cats, you'll want a breakaway collar — Tractive sells one separately for $15. The app walks you through setting up a virtual fence (geofence) for your home area. Total setup time: under 10 minutes.

Performance

GPS accuracy is excellent outdoors — consistently within 3-5 meters of the actual position. In dense urban areas with tall buildings, accuracy drops to 10-15 meters. Under heavy tree cover, expect 5-10 meter accuracy. The live tracking mode updates every 2-3 seconds, which is fast enough to follow a dog in real time on the map. The standard mode updates every 1-5 minutes to save battery. Indoor tracking does not work — GPS signals don't penetrate buildings.

App Experience

The Tractive app is polished and feature-rich. The map view shows your pet's current location and history trail. Virtual fences send push notifications when your pet enters or leaves a defined area. Activity tracking monitors daily movement, sleep, and rest — giving you a fitness profile for your pet. The app also shows battery level and sends low-battery alerts. One annoyance: the app constantly encourages you to upgrade to the Premium subscription ($8/mo vs basic $5/mo), but the basic plan has everything most users need.

Our Verdict

The Tractive GPS is the best all-around pet GPS tracker. For $49 upfront and $5/month, you get real-time tracking that actually works, reliable virtual fences, and useful activity monitoring. It's the best choice for dogs and outdoor cats that roam. Indoor-only cats or apartment pets don't need this level of tracking — consider an AirTag instead. Battery life is the main downside — expect to charge every 3-4 days. But the tracking quality and app experience make it worth the hassle.

Alternatives

  • Apple AirTag + Collar — No subscription but no real-time GPS tracking
  • Tabcat Cat Tracker — No subscription, RF-based, indoor only

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the Tractive GPS tracker?

Outdoors, accuracy is typically 3-5 meters. In urban canyons (between tall buildings), it drops to 10-15 meters. Under tree cover, expect 5-10 meters. GPS does not work indoors at all — walls block satellite signals. For indoor tracking, combine with an AirTag or Bluetooth tracker.

How long does the Tractive battery last?

With standard tracking (updates every few minutes), expect 4-5 days. In live tracking mode (updates every 2-3 seconds), battery drains in about 8-12 hours. Most users keep standard mode on and only activate live mode when actively looking for their pet. Charging takes about 2 hours.

Is the Tractive subscription worth it?

If you need real-time GPS tracking, yes — the subscription covers the cellular data needed to transmit location from the tracker to your phone. At $5/month for the basic plan, it's cheaper than Fi ($17-25/month) and comparable to other GPS trackers. Without the subscription, the tracker is useless.

Can I use Tractive without cellular service?

No. The Tractive tracker uses cellular networks to send GPS location to your phone. Without cellular coverage, it cannot transmit its position. However, the tracker works globally — it automatically connects to available cellular networks in 150+ countries.
